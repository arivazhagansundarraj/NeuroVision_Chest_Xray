About System

How PulmoScan turns an uploaded X-ray into a prediction.

1

Upload

The user uploads a chest X-ray in JPG, JPEG or PNG format (up to 10 MB).

2

Preprocessing

The image is converted to grayscale, resized to 128 × 128 pixels and scaled to the 0–1 range.

3

CNN model

A convolutional neural network extracts patterns from the lungs and outputs a probability score.

4

Prediction

A score of 0.5 or higher is classified as pneumonia, otherwise normal, and shown with its confidence.

Model architecture

Input 128×128×1Conv2D 32 + MaxPoolConv2D 64 + MaxPool Conv2D 128 + MaxPoolFlattenDense 128 (ReLU) Dropout 0.5Dense 1 (Sigmoid)

Trained with the Adam optimizer and binary cross-entropy loss.

System Note: PulmoScan is an AI-assisted research and educational system. Its predictions should not be considered a medical diagnosis.
